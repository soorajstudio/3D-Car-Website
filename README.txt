# Toy Car 3D Interactive Showcase (Cinematic Car Reel Camera Movement)

The 3D model website has been upgraded with **authentic automotive reel cinematography** — styled like high-speed FPV drone and gimbal chase car footage seen in modern car reels on Instagram, TikTok, and automotive commercials.

---

## What Makes This a "Car Reel" Movement (Not Just Rotating)
1. **The Car Stays Grounded with Realistic Stance**:
   Instead of the car spinning around on its axis like a microwave turntable, the car stays grounded with an aggressive posture and subtle steering angles.
2. **Cinematic Dutch Tilt / Roll Banking**:
   As the camera swoops through curves and past the doors, the camera rolls and banks (`-14°` to `+10°`) just like an FPV drone or banked chase gimbal.
3. **5 Automotive Reel Camera Shots Tied to Scroll**:
   - **Shot 1 · Low Bumper Track (Hero)**: Ground-level slider right by the front bumper and front wheel rim, looking up aggressively at the chrome grille.
   - **Shot 2 · Door Sill & Skirt Flyby (Materials)**: Low-slung side pass skimming right at wheel hub height, catching the clearcoat lacquer reflections and mirror line.
   - **Shot 3 · FPV Drone Roof Dive (Cards)**: Camera launches upward and banks into a steep diagonal overhead dive, looking down through the glass canopy into the cockpit.
   - **Shot 4 · Rear Chase Cam (Specs)**: Low-slung sweep around the rear bumper, spoiler silhouette, and tailpipe diffuser.
   - **Shot 5 · Crane Pull-Out Hero Reveal (Finale)**: Majestic elevated cinema crane pull-out framing the full car.
4. **Reactive Chassis Physics (Speed-Sensitive)**:
   - **Acceleration Squat & Braking Dive**: Scrolling faster down pitches the car backward on its rear axle (`pitch`); slowing down levels it forward.
   - **Centrifugal Body Roll**: As the camera swoops around corners, the car body leans into the turn.
   - **Engine Idle & Rumble**: Subtle road jitter and high-frequency engine idle vibration.
5. **▶ One-Click Auto-Reel Player**:
   Click **"▶ Play Cinematic Reel"** in the hero header or toolbar to automatically play the entire 14-second automotive reel sequence with cinematic pacing!

---

## Controls
- **Mouse Wheel / Trackpad**: Smoothly drives the reel camera through all shots without zooming.
- **`▶ Auto-Reel` Button**: Sit back and watch the cinematic reel play automatically.
- **Left-Click & Drag**: Freely orbit and inspect from any custom angle.
- **Zooming**:
  - Click **`🔍+`** and **`🔍−`** buttons on the bottom toolbar.
  - Or hold **`Ctrl + Mouse Wheel`**.
- **`🎬 Reel Cam: ON/OFF`**: Toggle between cinematic reel camera and free orbit.
- **`🌿 Mood`**: Switch between Emerald Studio, Racing Ruby, and Cyber Neon lighting rigs.
- **`↑ Top`**: Return smoothly to the top of the showcase.

## How to Run
- **Direct Double-Click**: Simply double-click `index.html` in Windows Explorer (embedded fallback ensures zero CORS errors).
- **One-Click Local Server**: Double-click `start_showcase.bat`.
